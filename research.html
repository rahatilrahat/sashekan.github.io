Job market paper

Trade Shocks, Intermediate Inputs and Labor Market Power

International trade · Labor economics · Industrial organization

Research summary

I study how import competition affects employers’ labor market power using firm-level data from Indian manufacturing. The paper examines wage markdowns and distinguishes the role of competition in output markets from access to imported intermediate inputs.

Other research

Labor Market Power and Structural Transformation

Research summary

This paper studies the relationship between labor market power and structural transformation. A model of worker selection across a competitive agricultural sector and a nonagricultural sector with monopsony links workers’ sectoral choices to observed productivity differences.
